About YPDC

Youth Professional Development Council

Who We Are

YPDC (Youth Professional Development Council) is a student-led council working under faculty supervision to develop leadership, professional and social skills among youth. Through six directorates we organize trainings, community projects, career events and cultural activities.

Established with the vision to create responsible, skilled and confident professionals, YPDC provides a platform where students learn by doing — managing real events, leading teams and serving the community.

Key Facts

  • Founded: 2018
  • Members: 1200+
  • Directorates: 6
  • Faculty Advisors: 8
  • Annual Events: 40+
