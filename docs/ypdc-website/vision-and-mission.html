Vision & Mission

Youth Professional Development Council

Our Vision

To be a leading youth council that nurtures ethical, skilled and socially responsible professionals who lead positive change in society.

Our Mission

To empower students through structured training, leadership roles, community service and industry exposure — building competence, character and confidence.

Core Values

Integrity

Honesty and ethics in every action.

Teamwork

Collaboration across directorates.

Innovation

Creative solutions to real problems.

Service

Giving back to the community.
